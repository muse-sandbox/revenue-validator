# IH-02 — One-time "final offer" discount interstitial after the winback window is exhausted

## Product context
The Ultimate Guitar app shows former subscribers a dedicated winback interstitial (a full-screen local paywall offering a return path to the premium tier at the standard ~$40/year price). Internal analysis had established that this winback interstitial only converts during its first few impressions; after roughly 2–5 shows, users who have not purchased effectively stop responding and fall back to ordinary ad monetization, the same as any free user. This experiment adds a terminal step to that sequence: a single, one-time "final" interstitial with a deeply discounted offer, shown only to former subscribers who have already sat through the standard winback window without converting.

## Pre-launch research & rationale
The team's confidence case had four pillars:

1. **Internal data — winback interstitials work on former subscribers.** An earlier winback interstitial experiment for lapsed subscribers was one of the strongest monetization touchpoints tested: on iOS its best arm delivered roughly +42.8% ARPU, +170.9% access conversion, and +37.8% charge conversion, lifting daily revenue from about $2,365 to $3,379; winback access conversion ran ~3.9% and charge conversion ~0.7%. The winback interstitial source converts at roughly 2–4%, versus under 0.2% for the standard free-user interstitial — a 10–20x gap — confirming former subscribers as the most convertible segment in the funnel.
2. **Internal data — the effective impression window is short.** A separate ad-volume-reduction analysis found ~60% of all interstitial conversions happen on the very first impression; on iOS ~90% happen within the first 2 impressions, on Android within the first 6. Beyond those thresholds extra impressions add essentially no subscription value — so users who did not convert after 2–5 winback shows need a fundamentally different offer, not a repeat.
3. **Industry pattern — "last chance" discounts for lapsed payers.** A major streaming service runs an explicit final-call discount aimed specifically at former paid subscribers (not trialists), on the reasoning that this audience has demonstrated purchase intent. A well-known subscription-infrastructure vendor's winback guidance likewise recommends reserving discounts for high-intent users and applying them only after the standard non-discounted offer cycle has run its course.
4. **Cannibalization risk assessed as low given sequencing.** The discount never precedes the standard offer: it appears only after the user has declined the ~$40/year offer across 2–5 impressions, and it is a one-time terminal touchpoint — limiting the risk of training users to wait for cheaper deals.

**Reach baselines (current state, per platform):** users who had seen 5+ winback splashes on iOS: 3,487, with 14 winback accesses (0.40%), ~3.5 winback charges, ~$82 winback revenue, ARPPU ~$23, winback-source ARPU ~$0.024; their total (all-source) numbers: 62 accesses (1.78%), 45 charges, ~$1,010 revenue, ARPU ~$0.29. Users with 8+ winback splashes on Android: 1,239, with 8 winback accesses (0.65%), ~2.4 charges, ~$64, ARPPU ~$27, winback ARPU ~$0.052; totals: 19 accesses (1.53%), 11 charges, ~$266, ARPU ~$0.21. A broader cut of users with 2+ winback splashes: iOS 7,030 (winback accesses 61 / 0.87%, charges 18, ~$432, winback ARPU ~$0.061; total ARPU ~$0.48), Android 2,677 (winback accesses 40 / 1.49%, charges 10, ~$266, winback ARPU ~$0.099; total ARPU ~$0.42). The exhausted segment currently produces essentially zero subscription revenue beyond these residual numbers.

**Open question logged pre-launch:** whether offer effectiveness differs by time since churn (0–3 months vs 12+ months); the team planned to analyze conversion by time-since-churn cohort after the experiment rather than segment on it up front.

## Hypothesis
As stated pre-launch: showing a final discounted offer to former subscribers who sat through the standard winback interstitial window without converting is expected to raise incremental ARPU per former subscriber by XX% (the specific expected lift was left unquantified in the document) without degrading the standard winback conversion rate — the reasoning being that this exhausted group currently contributes essentially no subscription revenue, so every purchase on the final offer is additive; the open question is whether the extra volume compensates for the cheaper price.

## Mechanics
- **Placement:** a new full-screen local interstitial on the tab surface, replacing the eligibility slot that would otherwise fall back to regular ad monetization.
- **Trigger:** eligibility check on tab visit — has the user viewed the winback interstitial, and was that viewing no later than the previous day? Only then does the final-offer interstitial become available (never sooner than the day after the winback view).
- **Timing / Frequency:** shown exactly once per user; a one-time, terminal touchpoint.
- **Audience segment:** former subscribers — users who previously had a paid premium subscription and currently have no active subscription — who have passed through the standard winback interstitial flow.
- **Platforms:** iOS and Android.
- **Offer-SKU structure:** the interstitial presents a promo code the user swipes/scratches to reveal; the revealed discount leads to a paywall offering a $19.99 plan (deep discount vs. the standard ~$40/year winback offer). View markers from the prior winback flow are re-linked so the old markers are invalidated when the new iteration starts.

## Experiment design
- **Arms (2):** A — control, legacy winback logic continues unchanged; B — after the winback window, the one-time final discounted interstitial is shown.
- **Primary metric:** incremental ARPU per former subscriber from the exhausted-winback segment (net-new revenue).
- **Guardrail:** the standard winback conversion rate must not drop.
- **Power / MDE / planned duration:** no formal power calculation was documented pre-launch (the design table remained an unfilled template).
- **Instrumentation plan:** activation event fired in both arms when the user hits the eligibility point; existing splash/paywall/purchase funnel events extended with a new source value for the final-offer interstitial plus coupon scratch/view/click events and a promo-code parameter.
