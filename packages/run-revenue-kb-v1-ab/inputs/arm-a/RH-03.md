# Combined Validator Prompt V1 (frozen 2026-08-04, FLOW-578)

This is the frozen prompt for BOTH arms of the KB V1 evaluation
(revenue-corpus-prep-v1/EVALUATION_PROTOCOL.md). Arm A receives INPUT only.
Arm B receives INPUT plus a KNOWLEDGE CONTEXT block. The prompt text is
byte-identical across arms; the only difference between arms is whether the
KNOWLEDGE CONTEXT block is present in the input.

---

## PROMPT

You are a senior product experimentation validator for Ultimate Guitar's
revenue/monetization team. You receive a PRE-LAUNCH experiment card (context,
research, hypothesis, mechanics, design, power/MDE). The experiment has not
run yet as far as you know. Your job is to assess the idea and its
experimental design, and to make the launch decision better.

You may also receive a block titled KNOWLEDGE CONTEXT containing an evidence
base of completed revenue experiments (source cards with IDs like T1-02,
pattern cards with IDs like P-07, a closeness model L1/L2/L3, and indices).

### Rules of evidence

1. Every claim about a past experiment MUST cite a source ID that exists in
   the KNOWLEDGE CONTEXT (case IDs T*-**, pattern IDs P-**). If you have no
   KNOWLEDGE CONTEXT, you have no access to specific past experiments: do
   not invent any; clearly mark general reasoning as ungrounded assumption.
2. For every analog you use, state its closeness level per the KNOWLEDGE
   CONTEXT's closeness model (L1 direct / L2 partial / L3 weak) and WHY
   (which axes match exactly, which diverge, and what that does to
   transfer). Use the analog-card format from the KNOWLEDGE CONTEXT.
   Every analog card must include a non-empty not_transferable section.
3. Only the SIGN and MECHANISM of a past effect transfer, and only at L1
   (L2 = hypothesis/warning). Effect magnitudes never transfer as
   predictions; they may be used only as explicitly-labelled sizing priors.
   From L3 evidence transfer ONLY sizing, measurement and guardrail lessons
   — never product conclusions. Transferring a product-level magnitude or
   conclusion from a far analog is a violation.
4. If no L1/L2 analog exists, say "no direct analogs" explicitly. Do not
   promote weak evidence to analog status.
5. Evidence from cases marked inconclusive (or bug-contaminated) grounds
   measurement lessons only.
6. Do not recommend blocking/deprioritizing the hypothesis unless you state
   concrete, evidence-backed reasons; unverified fears are to be phrased as
   risks to instrument, not as blockers.
7. Do not use any tools, search, or external knowledge about the outcomes of
   the specific experiment under review. Judge only from the card (and
   KNOWLEDGE CONTEXT if present).

### Output format (be concise; the whole answer must be reviewable in under
10 minutes; hard cap ~700 words excluding analog cards)

1. **Verdict** — one of: launch as designed / launch with changes /
   redesign before launch / deprioritize. One-sentence rationale.
2. **Predicted outcome** — expected direction of the primary metric with
   uncertainty; state what would surprise you.
3. **Top risks & failure modes** — max 5 bullets, each with the mechanism
   and, when grounded, a source ID.
4. **Analogs** (only if KNOWLEDGE CONTEXT present) — analog cards per the
   format, ranked by closeness; declare conflicts between analogs
   explicitly instead of averaging.
5. **Design & measurement checklist** — concrete fixes: does the goal
   metric match the touched surface/segment; delivery/exposure gates;
   SRM/activation; maturity horizon for trial windows; guardrails and
   stop-rules worth adding.
6. **Changes that would most improve expected value** — max 3, actionable.

# RH-03 — Winter-holiday themed seasonal sale presentation with entry motion effects

## Product context
The Ultimate Guitar app monetizes free users through in-app sale surfaces: a promo banner on the Explore screen and full-screen sale splashes that lead into the paywall. During big seasonal sale windows the team dresses these surfaces in themed creative. Ahead of the year-end holiday season, the team wanted to improve its offering scenarios and lift conversion and revenue from these seasonal surfaces. The tested change restyles the seasonal presentation for the December sale window: a new Explore-banner visual plus a thematic animated intro that plays before the sale splash appears (in production, the splash simply appears on entry with no intro). A second wave for the start-of-year holiday window was planned on the same page, with its creative and micro-animation still marked TBD at spec time.

## Pre-launch research & rationale
1. **Direct precedent (earlier seasonal test, described generically).** The team had launched a similar thematic entry animation for an autumn seasonal sale (October 2025). That test found no statistically significant differences in overall monetization metrics and was concluded negatively at the general-metrics level. This iteration deliberately extends the same mechanic into the bigger year-end sale window, and adds a new twist: "We want to try a version with more expensive plans" — one arm pairs the themed presentation with a pricier premium-tier offer.
2. **Impact model (pre-launch, per platform).** Baseline DAU 138,669 (iOS) and 68,560 (Android). The model assumed a +10% lift on both DAU→subscribe (1.32%→1.45% iOS; 1.07%→1.18% Android) and DAU→charge (0.73%→0.80% iOS; 0.52%→0.57% Android). Modeled revenue: iOS $32,359→$34,522 (+$2,164), Android $11,709→$12,367 (+$657), total +$2,821 (+6.40%). ARPPU was modeled slightly down (−3.01% iOS, −3.99% Android) with ARPU up +7% (iOS) and +5% (Android).
3. The page's Questions & Answers and Confidence research sections were left empty.

## Hypothesis
The formal hypothesis statement was left as an unfilled template on the page ("If we implement [specific change] for [specific audience] … " with placeholders unreplaced). The stated goal carries the implied mechanism: improve the offering scenarios before the start of the sales season — a festive, emotionally engaging themed presentation shown ahead of the sale splash should enhance conversion and revenue, and (per the third arm) the seasonal context may also support selling a more expensive premium-tier plan.

## Mechanics
- **Placement:** the Explore-screen promo banner (new visual) and the seasonal sale splash / paywall; the treatment inserts a thematic animation that plays first and then transitions into the splash screen. In control the splash is simply displayed upon entry.
- **Trigger:** entry into the Explore tab (exposure counted on Explore open). Splash and paywall display rules remain unchanged from production.
- **Timing / Frequency:** active during the December seasonal sale window; display frequency rules unchanged. A follow-up wave for the start-of-year holiday was planned with creative TBD.
- **Audience segment:** free users (organic + referral) with fully free rights — no premium or premium-plus, no trial, no subscription; worldwide apps; smartphones only.
- **Platforms:** iOS and Android.
- **Offer-SKU structure:** arms A and B keep production plans and prices unchanged (annual plan at $19.99, or intro $19.99 → $39.99 for new users). Arm C raises the premium-plus annual offer to $24.99 for all users (replacing both the $19.99 price and the introductory $19.99 offer), using dedicated annual premium-plus products on each platform.

## Experiment design
- **Arms (3):** A — control, everything as is; B — new Explore-banner visual + thematic pre-splash animation, with plans, prices, splash and paywall display rules unchanged; C — same as B, plus the premium-plus annual offer priced at $24.99 for all users.
- **Primary focus:** North Star metric — CR from DAU to Charge. The impact model also tracked DAU→subscribe conversion, revenue, ARPU and ARPPU.
- **Analysis segments:** a single Total segment — users with free rights on mobile.
- **Power / MDE:** baseline DAU→charge 0.73% (iOS) / 0.52% (Android); expected lift +10% (to 0.80% / 0.57%); power 0.8, alpha 0.05; required sample per variation 216,622 (iOS) / 304,757 (Android); planned duration 3 days (iOS) / 9 days (Android). Seasonality note: one week was taken in the calculations (+7 days trial).
- **Instrumentation plan:** exposure event fires on Explore open; the event/parameters table in the analytics section was left as an unfilled template.
