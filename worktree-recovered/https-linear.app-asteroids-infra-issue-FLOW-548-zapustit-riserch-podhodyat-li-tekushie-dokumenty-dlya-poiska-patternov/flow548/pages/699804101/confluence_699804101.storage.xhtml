Decision

Results

  • Monetization — [UG_WEB / Apple payments only / Variation 2]

    • ARPU metric: +246%, revenue per user up

    • Payment funnel CRs (Access CR, Charge CR): +219%-249%, more users reach and complete payment

  • Monetization — [UG_WEB / Total / Variation 2] AOV: +3.26%, average order value up

Conclusion GreenSuccess

  • Achieved all metrics, specified in the hypothesis. Get another working payment method
  • The Apple-pay-eligible segment shows very large, highly significant gains in ARPU and key payment conversion rates, consistent with the one‑click checkout hypothesis
  • On total traffic the only significant movement is a modest AOV lift, likely because Apple‑pay‑eligible users are a small share of overall visitors
  • Retention and tab view metrics show no significant changes, suggesting no engagement harm
  • Roll out Apple Pay in Chrome on macOS for all eligible checkouts (courses, songbooks, upsells, offers)
