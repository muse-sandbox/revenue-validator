Decision

Conclusion

No statistically significant effects were detected on iOS or Android across monetization, retention, or tab-view metrics (p ≥ 0.05). Given the absence of clear monetization lift and some sizable but non-significant negative trends on Android engagement (e.g., tab views per user down ~40%–44%, 1d retention down ~20%), do not roll out the current banner. Continue testing or iterate the UX/copy and targeting to reduce potential disruption, especially since the banner is shown once per session when users hit paywalled features or open official/pro tabs.
