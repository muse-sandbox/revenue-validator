# Research and Context

Goal: increase revenue and conversion from the mobile web

Foundation : 

introduce monetization mechanisms (microtransactions, rewarded access etc.) that better fit mobweb user's behavior and expectations 

we’re doing this now because only ~1% of daily users install the app , leaving a significant untapped audience

### Research results

There have been attempts to sell our basic plans, but they may prove too expensive for the mobile web audience. We want to try selling features separately:

One-time, single-use payments, 1-day access unlocks, feature unlocks 

$0.99/day or $0.29/hour feature

24-hour access to a song

package sales: 5 songs for $3.99

Daytime access to everything

Hybrid with ads: $0.99 or watch ads

Purchase of features or free version in the app

etc.

# Hypothesis

If we introduce microtransactions for mobile web users, we expect that more than 1.5% of the audience will click on the paid access button, which will prove that the monetization model through microtransactions is viable.

Test Description:

one-sided test,

H0: p < p0 VS H1: p >= p0,

p0 (threshold) = 1.5%,

p1(expected real conversion) = 1.6%.

Audience Active users on mob WEB

0.13% VS 1.5% target

Verdict ❌  FAILED

The new mechanism cannibalised 45.25% of clicks on Install and 54.40% of total app installs, which is statistically significant for both metrics.

With a large cannibalisation of installs, the new mechanism brought too few clicks on the microtransaction button ("Unlock for 0.99$") - 0.13% vs. 1.5% of the planned one.

#### Conclusion

The coefficients used in the model turned out to be much higher than the actual values; with such views and clicks, the economics will not add up

We close the iteration as FAILED

The next step is to try to expand the funnel at the start using the bottom scroll splash source + additionally test interest in viewing the ad tab

## Decision

Platform(s)

Audience
10% of all WEB mobile not Asia DAU

Period
   –   

Result 0.19% VS 1.5% target

Verdict ❌  FAILED

The new mechanisms cannibalised:

"microtransaction" button: 45% of clicks on Install, 57% of total app installs, 29% of fact revenue.

"watch ad" button: 35% of clicks on Install and 43% of total app installs, 25% of fact revenue.

We have too few clicks on the " microtransaction" button ("Unlock for $0.99") - 0.19% (or 74 users) versus 1.5% of the planned one, that is not enough to even cover the cannibalised installs.

"Watch ad" button has a higher CTR - 2.77% (or 1103 users), but this is not enough to even cover the cannibalised installs.

#### Conclusion

Still haven't reached the number of clicks and button presses specified in the model with purchase

Viewers interact better with ads, but the overall economics are even worse, so we are not testing this branch further

We close the iteration as FAILED

Next, we will try to show the splash screen with the microtransaction button to the maximum number of viewers

Verdict ❌  FAILED

If we assume that users who clicked on the microtransactions would not have installed the app later — since they would have continued using the functionality on mobile web — then the new mechanism would have cannibalized 42% of installs and 34% of in-app revenue , while the total revenue including estimated microtransaction revenue would have decreased by 5.7% .

We are closing this iteration and the project as a whole as FAILED

Next, we will conduct qualitative research on the mobile web audience through user interviews, which may yield additional insights for ideas involving microtransactions
