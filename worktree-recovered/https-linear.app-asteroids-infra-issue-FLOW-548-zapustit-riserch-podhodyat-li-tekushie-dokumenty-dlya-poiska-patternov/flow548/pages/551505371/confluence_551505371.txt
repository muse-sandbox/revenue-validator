# Hypothesis

If we target the audience that didn't opt for the songbook upsell initially with a follow-up offer, we can gain an additional 10 songbook accesses per day (increase by 20%)

# A/B test plan

# Description of the Solution & Mockups

Solution

Adding to upsell flow the option for one-click purchase (essentially skipping the checkout step)

When clicking on the CTA in the modal, display the paywall without requiring payment details (one-click purchase checkout)

On it, we display the offer details and payment information

By default, the payment method is set to the card used for the recent PRO purchase

If desired, you can add a new card or edit the current one

For PRO users who declined the upsell, we replace the current banner leading to the Songbooks section with a banner featuring a 24-hour timer to emphasize the limited-time offer

the banner will be non-closable

after 24 hours, we revert to the regular banner leading to the Songbooks section

When the banner is clicked, a modal window with the offer appears, explaining the details of the offer, price, and benefits

When the CTA button in the modal window is clicked, it redirects to the one-click purchase checkout

After purchasing PRO, we initiate a one-click checkout purchase for Songbooks

After the trial ends or is cancelled — we return the old flow, without the possibility of purchasing in one click

At the same time, there is no possibility to take a repeat trial, only instant plans
