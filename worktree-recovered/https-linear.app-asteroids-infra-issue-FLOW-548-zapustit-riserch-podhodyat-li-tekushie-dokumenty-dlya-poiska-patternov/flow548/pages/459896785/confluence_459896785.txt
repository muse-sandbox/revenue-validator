# Hypothesis

Increase local prices and make them equal to USD price in local price and check how it affect final revenue and amount of users.  Launch experiment only in locations where price will be changed.

# A/B test plan

Base value of the metric: APRU except USD , EUR , CHF , GBP $1.14

Lift, % 10%

Minimal detectable effect 0.0214

Probability of true-positive (power) 0.8

Probability of false-positive (p-value) 0.05

Sample size (for all the variations) 68895

How many days it'll take to conduct the test 45

# Description of the Solution & Mockups

### Solution

In the test variations, we offer increased price everywhere except USD , EUR , CHF , GBP

B: increased price - we’re aligning all local prices to USD.

Adjust the discount amount everywhere it appears

We've decided to create a new list of plan IDs for a new "tier" because we need options to separate plans with different local prices. So, we'll be rolling out new plan IDs and plan codes for all countries, regardless of the fact that prices will only change for some merchants.
