# Research and Context

Goal:  reduce subscription churn and increase upsell conversions by clarifying whether users intend to cancel only Pro or all subscriptions

Foundation :

— the study highlighted the problem that users do not understand that the songbook+course bundle purchased during the upsell is a separate subscription

user reviews left via support also mention this

due to this misunderstanding, they do not cancel the trial for the purchased bundle and then process a refund through refand. the idea behind the project is to make it clear when canceling what exactly the user is canceling and whether they want to cancel the purchased upsell bundle along with the subscription

### Research results

Refunds & Disputes research shows that most refunds in upsells come from users who canceled their Pro subscription trial period. From this, we can conclude that a large number of refunds come from users who canceled their subscription, even though they had already paid for the bundle, and were dissatisfied with it 

#### Usability research

Usability research revealed that users did not understand that they were taking out several different subscriptions, which also had to be canceled separately.

Screen Conclusion Example reviews

  When users want to cancel, they don't understand that they have to cancel 2 subscriptions and cancel only one I now have   two subscriptions, and I thought I just had one . But now do I have to cancel my other subscription? Um, it'd be my understanding that I have totally canceled all my subscriptions, so, oh, okay. [She didn't cancel the second]

# Hypothesis

If we add a clarification to the cancellation funnel for users who have purchased upsells, specifying what exactly they want to cancel, we will reduce the number of refunds by 55% and improve the upsell process as a whole, this will work because we will remove ambiguities that may have confused users
