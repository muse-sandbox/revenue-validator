Decision

Results

  • [UG_WEB / Total / Variation 2]

    • ARPU: +42%, increased revenue per user

    • Access CR: +38.69%, higher entry into the paid flow

    • Charge CR: +48.28%, higher conversion to charge

  • [UG_WEB / Without registration splash / Variation 2] ARPPU: +14.32%, increased revenue per paying user

Conclusion GreenSuccess

  • The shortened funnel and the new, review-driven paywall-style offer substantially improved monetization: ARPU rose ~45% alongside strong gains in аccess and сharge conversion 
  • This aligns with the hypothesis that surfacing the plan upfront (and using social proof via rotating reviews) increases motivation and reduces friction before the paywall
  • Retention and tab-view engagement showed no significant change, and cancellation/refund metrics did not significantly worsen, suggesting no immediate downsides
  • Roll outed variation 2 

Next steps

  • See if the effect wears off over time

  • QA and harden social authorization (Google/Facebook) across the shortened flow and paywall to avoid drop-offs in identity-related steps

  • Follow-up A/Bs to isolate drivers: compare reviews vs. benefit-based messaging; optionally test the “ambulance” on first close and the 24h persistent banner as separate reinforcements
