# Research and Context

Project goal:  

Generate incremental PRO+ subscription revenue by testing a new pricing presentation approach. The goal is to evaluate whether communicating the offer as an absolute price performs better than the long-used relative discount framing .

Foundation :

Relative discount framing: “90% off”

Absolute price framing: “Annual access just for $24.99. Our final offer.”

Our current core offer — “90% off” — has remained largely unchanged for 2 or 3 years. While the discount level is strong, users may have become accustomed to this framing.

To validate whether pricing communication impacts conversion, we plan to test two different approaches:

The hypothesis is that an absolute price may reduce cognitive effort and make the value more tangible , potentially improving conversion compared to a percentage-based discount that users have seen repeatedly.

Overall conclusion:  

Despite no meaningful differences in core email performance metrics (OR, CTR), variant B significantly outperformed variant A in downstream business metrics. 

We observed:

+46.9% increase in accesses (115 → 169)

+36.6% increase in revenue ($3,096.81 → $4,231.28)

+69.9% uplift in click-to-access conversion rate (3.03% → 5.15%)

This indicates that while engagement at the email level remained similar, the quality and conversion intent of traffic in variant B were substantially higher , leading to stronger business impact.

 

Next steps:

Test other markets to validate consistency of results
