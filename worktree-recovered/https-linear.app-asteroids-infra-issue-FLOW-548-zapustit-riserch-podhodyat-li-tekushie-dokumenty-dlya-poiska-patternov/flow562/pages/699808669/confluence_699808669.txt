Goal: generate additional revenue on sales from interactive tabs

Foundation : to make the design of Guitar Pro and Power tabs similar to each other, it will improve the user experience, and closing the feature will give us new revenue

### Research results

Guitar Pro tabs

Power tabs

## Decision

Results

[UG_WEB / Total / Variation 2]

ARPU: +448%, revenue per user strongly up after paywall and promo banner.

ARPPU: +79%, higher spend among paying users.

Access CR, %: +292%, more users accessing interactive tabs after removing registration dependence and opening instruments.

Charge CR, %: +206%, more charges per member driven by the paywall + banner funnel.

Charge -> 14d refund, %: (0% -> 22.2%), sharp spike in refunds post‑charge.

Tab view 60s, %: +1.9%, slight lift in short viewing.

Tab view 300s–600s, %: -3.7% to -4.0%, lower long‑form engagement likely due to fixed banner and feature locking.

Tab view per user: +14.4%, more total tab views per user.

Tab view 60s per user: +7.0%, more short views per user.

Conclusion 
[macro]
Green Success
[/macro]

Hiding the free functionality behind the paywall got a tangible new ~30 accesses per day

Overall, the new layout doesn't sag on the rest of the metrics

The paywall + fixed promo banner significantly improved monetization (ARPU, ARPPU, access and charge conversion), consistent with opening instruments and removing registration dependence

Long‑duration tab engagement (300s–600s) dropped, likely because locking most functionality behind a paywall and keeping a fixed banner above the player disrupts deep usage. Retention was unchanged

Roll outed test variation #2 

At the same time, due to hiding the download button tab received a lot of negative feedback and appeal to support

Next steps

in next iteration we returned download button

## Decision

Results

Monetization — [UG_WEB / Total / Variation 2] access cr, %: -46.1%, conversion to access fell

Monetization — [UG_WEB / Total / Variation 2] charge -> 14d refund, %: +800.0%, refund rate spiked (on a likely low base)

Tab View — [UG_WEB / Total / Variation 2] tab view 120s–600s, %: +2.1% to +2.7%, higher share of long views

Tab View — [UG_WEB / Total / Variation 2] tab view per user: -5.5%, fewer tab views per user

Conclusion
[macro]
Red FAIL
[/macro]

Lost all the gain from the first iteration

Reinstating the Download button and the Corrections link appears to siphon attention away from the monetization path: access conversion dropped sharply and the 14‑day refund rate increased, while retention did not change

The Interactive Tabs funnel supports this diagnosis: the “Upgrade Open → Plans View” step fell from ~10% to ~4–5% and members→purchase roughly halved, consistent with users diverting to the new links

Although the share of longer views rose modestly, these engagement gains do not offset the monetization and refund risks

Roll outed anyway due to multiple users requests about tabs downloading
