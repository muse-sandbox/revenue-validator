Exp #7622 accumulated ≈ 73.5k bucketed users (≈ 24.5k per variation) in 22 days and detected ARPU +17.25% (p = 0.002; mature readings +22.8%, p = 0.011 and +24.2%, p = 0.001) and Retention 14d −1.60 pp (p = 0.004). The binding metric for this iteration is the Retention 14d guardrail (pp-level effects), which this very allocation already resolved; ARPU-level effects need less sample.
