Prior evidence: the #7622 first-session research below. Statistical robustness of the prior evidence:

  • The Retention 14d decline is significant on the full bucketed cohort: iOS B −1.60 pp (p = 0.004), Android B −2.26 pp (p = 7·10⁻⁵), Android C −2.39 pp (p = 2.4·10⁻⁵). In the dominant segment — users who reached the paywall without purchasing (81% of the cohort) — the decline is −1.79 pp (p = 0.0037).

  • The ARPU lift is reproduced in two independent mature readings: +22.8% (p = 0.011) and +24.2% (p = 0.001). It comes from conversion (members → subscribers +13.1%), not from the average check (AOV +3.1%, not significant).

  • Randomization at the exposure boundary is clean: no sample ratio mismatch was detected.

  • Composition decomposition: variation B brings +0.75 pp more early subscribers, and they retain better than average, so the composition shift masks the harm (its contribution is −0.23 pp); the mix-adjusted decline is −1.82 pp, larger than the raw one.
