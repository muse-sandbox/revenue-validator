Pitch

Context & Idea

Goal. Preserve the monetization uplift of the previous first-session Variation B while recovering most of the Retention 14d loss.

Foundation. The relaunched guided first-song flow (exp #7622, Variation B) increased total ARPU by sending more users into the Official Tabs purchase funnel. The lift came from conversion, not AOV, while Retention 14d and tab-view engagement declined.

Idea. After the first Official Tab is shown, give the user useful context inside the tab before Paywall 2. The experience branches by the format selected during onboarding (Tabs vs Chords).

Questions & Answers

Open decisions that block launch:

  1. Demo composition: approve compact Simplify + Transpose or the multi-step Backing track / Strumming / Arrows / Simplify / Play flow (see Solution, "Demo shape: unresolved").
  2. Paywall personalization: the hypothesis says Paywall 2 is personalized by selected settings, while the detailed solution only says Paywall 2. Specify what changes by Tabs/Chords and by selected settings.
  3. Experiment arms: decide whether a third arm preserving Variation B is required for a direct mechanism comparison.
  4. Retention guardrail: approve the maximum acceptable Retention 14d loss versus control; −0.5 pp is only a working value.
  5. Canonical exposure event: the document alternates between the existing Tour Post Decline Gift Offer Close boundary and a new App Experiment Start event. Choose one implementation and admin configuration.
  6. Statistical design: finalize MDE, power, alpha, sample size, and duration after decisions 3–4.

Confidence research

Decision-relevant evidence from exp #7622:

  • The monetization gain is real and volume-driven. Mature readings reproduced an ARPU lift of +22.8% and +24.2%; members → subscribers increased by about 13%, while AOV did not change significantly.
  • The retention loss is real. On the full bucketed cohort, Retention 14d declined by 1.60 pp on iOS. The decline is concentrated among users who reached the paywall without purchasing (81% of the cohort; −1.79 pp).
  • Randomization at the exposure boundary is clean; the change in user composition does not explain the difference and partially masks it.

What I checked

Research base: First session 3rd iteration. Findings that affect the solution:

  1. More explanation alone did not solve the problem. Variation C added information and choice but did not improve retention relative to B. The awareness-gap mechanism is therefore unconfirmed.
  2. Keep the flow short. The format-choice step loses about 1% of users. The largest later loss is Paywall 2 → Tab View 60s: 34–40% do not reach 60 seconds. After paywall dismissal, the chooser improves this transition, so the flow should add no repeated pressure there.
  3. Feature demand differs by chosen format. About 78% choose Chords and 22% choose Tabs. Chords users show comparatively stronger demand for Simplify and Transpose; Tabs demand is concentrated in Smartscroll and Autoscroll. This supports branching the experience by format.
  4. Launch on iOS only. iOS harm is concentrated in Intermediate users, but level remains an analytical slice rather than an eligibility rule. Android showed retention loss in both Beginner and Intermediate and is out of scope for this iteration.

Reach & Impact

Reach

Audience. New first-session users with free rights on UGT_IOS. Estimated full-rollout reach: approximately 7,700 exposed users/day.

Is the audience sufficient? Yes: exp #7622 collected approximately 24,500 users/variation in 22 days and resolved both the ARPU and the Retention 14d effects.

Largest possible segment. Keep organic, referral, paid, and reinstalls eligible. Do not gate by country or user level — level stays an analytical slice in the readout.

Impact

Target metrics. Goal — Total ARPU / exposed at 14 days. Guardrail (binding) — Retention 14d. Proxies — members → subscribers, members → buyers, Tab View 60s, and demo completion/failure.

A/B model = forecast.

  • Total ARPU / exposed: hold approximately the Variation B level, about +20% versus control.
  • Retention 14d: improve versus Variation B and remain within the approved margin from control (working margin: no worse than −0.5 pp; not final, must be approved before launch).
  • AOV: flat; the hypothesis does not depend on check growth.
  • No additional traffic is expected from the change (first-session flow only, no acquisition effect).

Rationale. The test reuses the Variation B funnel and only adds in-tab context before Paywall 2, so the conversion mechanism is preserved. Main risk: the demo step dampens paywall pressure and ARPU regresses toward control — hence the goal is holding the B level, not a further lift.

Hypothesis

If we show useful in-tab context branched by chosen format after the first Official Tab and before Paywall 2, for new free users in their first session on iOS, we expect Total ARPU / exposed to hold approximately the Variation B level (about +20% vs control) without a drop in Retention 14d beyond the approved margin from control, because the user reaches the paywall after receiving value and with personal context.

Description of the Solution & Mockups

Solution

Stable flow

  1. Paywall 1.
  2. Gift offer; closing it is the experiment exposure point.
  3. Pick the first song.
  4. Choose how to play: Tabs or Chords.
  5. Open the Official Tab.
  6. Branch by chosen format: Tabs — no paid-catalog setup step; proceed to Paywall 2 with playback value as the leading message. Chords — show a short in-tab setup/demo, then Paywall 2.
  7. If Paywall 2 is dismissed, go to Explore. Do not add another sales prompt.

Constraints that apply to any selected demo

  • The path is linear; there is no back navigation inside the demo.
  • The user cannot exit the flow without reaching Paywall 2. Skipping all demo steps reproduces the prior Variation B path.
  • Closing a tooltip changes the step to a Continue state; it does not close the flow.
  • Skip a step when its feature is unavailable for the selected song.
  • Fail forward on component or download errors; never leave the user on a broken overlay or dead end.
  • Any temporary paid-feature unlock is client-side and must not modify the user's rights.
  • While a guided overlay is active, unrelated tab controls are disabled.

Demo shape: unresolved

The document currently contains two incompatible versions:

OptionDescriptionSupporting evidence / risk
Compact setupOne Simplify + Transpose surface for Chords usersMatches the stated selection rule and format-demand research; minimizes funnel cost. No detailed flow or mockup is specified.
Multi-step demoBacking track → Strumming → Arrows → Simplify → PlayFully described in the Solution and Figma sections, but conflicts with the compact setup decision and omits Transpose. More steps may increase funnel loss.

Do not begin implementation until one option is approved and the Figma flow matches it.

If the multi-step option is selected, preserve these conditional behaviors:

  • Backing track, Strumming, and Simplify steps are skipped when unavailable.
  • Simplify can be toggled before Continue; show the changed chords without requiring scroll.
  • The final Play step runs for up to 8 seconds. Pause or another tap ends it early and opens Paywall 2.

Out of scope

  • Android.
  • Gating or separate product flows by user level, country, or acquisition channel.
  • Acquisition changes or additional traffic.
  • Repeated paywall pressure after Paywall 2 dismissal.
  • Writing temporary demo access to user rights.

QA

Critical failure paths:

  • No demo features available: skip the entire demo and continue Official Tab → Paywall 2 without broken overlays.
  • Individual feature unavailable: skip only that step and continue in the approved sequence.
  • Download or component error: fail forward and record the fallback reason.
  • Tabs branch: never enter a Chords-only setup step.
  • Tooltip close / Continue: neither action may bypass Paywall 2.
  • Paywall dismissal: go to Explore with no repeated sales prompt.
  • Temporary unlock: works only inside the demo and never changes persistent rights.
  • Experiment exposure: fires once, from the same code path, for all variations.
