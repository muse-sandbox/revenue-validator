Sized 2026-07-31 on the measured exp #7622 final reading (UGT_IOS, segment Total, result dt 2026-07-10 — the same calculator pipeline the readout will use; inputs and derivations: design calc). α 0.05 two-sided, power 0.8, 2 variations (A control + B setup path) 50/50, no multiplicity correction. Goal and proxy lifts are the Hypothesis targets (hold the variation-B level). The ARPU sizing uses the measured user-level SD $4.21, derived from the stored CI of the exp #7622 ARPU diff (SE $0.0380, members 24,685 / 24,316). Baselines are the sloperator reading; the Impact section quotes $0.66 / 37.58% from the research bucketed-cohort definitions — the sizing is insensitive to that difference.

The guardrail is the binding metric. It is sized to detect a −1.0 pp Retention 14d drop (lift -2.37%) — between the working acceptable bound (−0.5 pp) and the measured B-level harm (−2.11 pp on this metric definition). Certifying −0.5 pp non-inferiority directly would take ≈152,849 users per variation — infeasible. At the binding sample the design also detects an ARPU lift of 12.6% (the goal needs 20%) and sees the full B-level retention harm with power ≈ 1.

Duration is the calendar run = enrollment + 14 (the 14-day ARPU / retention windows must close) + 2 settle. Enrollment is read off the measured exp #7622 accrual at the exposure event Tour Post Decline Gift Offer Close (version-adoption ramp ≈ 2.4k/day on days 1–10, plateau ≈ 5.0k/day, at the exp #7622 slot share; a new version-gated experiment repeats this ramp). Binding enrollment is 23 days for 76,293 bucketed users — the measured 22-day curve reached 73,525, the small tail is extrapolated at the plateau rate. Adding a third variation (B as is, for a direct mechanism comparison) at an equal split pushes binding enrollment to ≈31 days (calendar ≈47) — decide per the open design question before slot booking.
