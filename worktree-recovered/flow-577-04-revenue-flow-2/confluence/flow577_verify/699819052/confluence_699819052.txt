# Research and Context

Goal: Determine the current impact of promo/sale mechanics on premium user acquisition

Foundation

For users without Pro access, we operate a "permanent seasonal sales" mechanic - the same offer but with different visuals depending on the season. The seasonal offer has two main placements:

Permanent banner on Explore with two scenarios: introductory 50% discount in the first 24h after install and permanent 50% discount after 24h

Splash screens shown on app entry (limited by display frequency and number of shows per promotion, and cannot be shown on the same day as onboarding tour or app update tour)

For Splashes, there's a concept of key promotion - a seasonal promotion code that resets all limits when changed

Historically, switching promotions to a new season gave us a sales boost, but the last 2 promotions (spring and summer) no longer show the usual growth

In this project, we want to test current promotion effectiveness and its impact on ARPU, and determine next steps for sales direction development based on results
