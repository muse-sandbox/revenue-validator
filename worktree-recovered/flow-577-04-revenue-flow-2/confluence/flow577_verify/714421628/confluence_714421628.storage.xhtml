Decision

Results

  • [UG_WEB / Total / variation 2]

    • arpu: +12.1%, revenue per user grew

    • trial -> charge, %: +32.0%, more trials convert to paid

    • subscription -> charge, %: +8.6%, higher billing success

    • charge -> 14d cancel, %: -19.2%, early churn fell

    • Tab View metrics (60s–180s): -1.3% to -1.6%, reduced viewing depth

  • [UG_WEB / Total / variation 3]

    • arpu: +19.2%, revenue per user grew

    • charge cr, %: +15.1%, higher checkout-to-charge conversion

    • trial -> charge, %: +28.9%, more trials convert to paid

    • charge -> 14d cancel, %: -20.0%, early churn fell

    • Tab View metrics (60s–300s): -1.1% to -1.4%, reduced viewing depth

    • retention 1d, %: -4.8%, short-term retention down

    • retention 7d, %: -2.5%, mid-term retention down

    • retention 14d, %: -2.1%, mid-term retention down

  • [UG_WEB / Print sources only / variation 2]

    • arpu: +25.6%, revenue per user grew

    • trial -> charge, %: +36.7%, more trials convert to paid

  • [UG_WEB / Print sources only / variation 3]

    • arpu: +33.8%, revenue per user grew

    • charge cr, %: +27.8%, higher checkout-to-charge conversion

    • charge -> 14d cancel, %: -35.1%, early churn fell

Conclusion GreenSuccess

  • Both treatments tied to the instant introductory offer (64.99 → 99.99 on the second cycle), new checkout disclaimer, and new paywalls improved monetization.
  • Variation 3 (C — three-level paywall) drives the largest gains: ARPU up ~19% overall
  • 2% of offers were wrongfully misinterpreted by backend as week offers and charged after a week
  • Recommendation: Roll out, but staged. Prefer C (three-level paywall)

Next steps

  • fix problems with into subscriptions
  • View the number of debits per user  
  • relaunch with new prices
