Research and Context

Goal

Offer long-term Pro users a special anniversary upgrade deal (Pro + Courses at a discount) to increase monetization. The goal is to boost ARPU among active Pro subscribers by leveraging a personalized upgrade offer.

Foundation

Current state: Pro subscribers only see standard upsell offers for additional subscriptions (e.g., Courses / Sing).

Problem: Long-term Pro subscribers do not receive personalized, milestone-based offers, which reduces upsell efficiency.

Proposed change: Introduce Anniversary splash screens shown at specific Pro subscription milestones (30, 90, 180, 365 days).

Research results

Previous internal experiment: Anniversary splash for free-tier users boosted conversion rates, proving the format resonates.

External references: Subscription-based apps widely use milestone-based offers with success (Headspace, Flo).

Hypothesis

If we display an Anniversary splash to Pro subscribers at 30/90/180/365 days after subscription start, we expect ARPU from premium (Pro) DAU to increase by 70–90% without negatively affecting retention or user experience. This is because milestone-triggered, personalized offers have historically performed better than generic ones.

Results

    • ARPU: +3914.1% (growth)

    • access cr, %: +3000+% (growth)

    • charge cr, %: +3000+% (growth)

    • ARPU: +600% (growth)

  • [UGT_ANDROID / Total / variation 2]

    • ARPU: +1000% (growth)

    • access cr, %: +800% (growth)

    • charge cr, %: +1300% (growth)

    • subscription -> charge, %: +60% (growth)

  • [UGT_ANDROID / Active Trials (for bug test) / variation 2]

    • charge cr, %: +800% (growth)

Conclusion

  • The personalized Anniversary splash (given higher priority over Sale Offer) drove very large monetization lifts across iOS and Android without measurable impact on retention or tab-view engagement.
  • This suggests the higher-priority splash successfully converted eligible Pro subscribers.
  • However, the Active Trials cohort (which should be excluded per design) shows significant exposure effects
    • This points to a targeting/eligibility bug (Anniversary gift funnel launches right after trial)
    • but overall it had no affect on monetization
      • only 6 subscriptions from iOS and 26 on Android
  • Recommendation: roll out with fix: remove users with trials from anniversary funnel

Next steps

  • fix bug with trials: because anniversary calculates through expiration date which is in 7-14 days for trials
