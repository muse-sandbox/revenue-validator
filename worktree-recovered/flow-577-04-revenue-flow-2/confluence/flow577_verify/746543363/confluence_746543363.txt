# Pitch

## Context & Idea

Goal

Increase revenue through greater variability in sales scenarios and offers

Foundation

Currently, we use a single banner for sales and offers on Explore, with space reserved for it at the top of the Explore screen. The current situation presents us with a number of limitations:

Promo banner size - in its current form, it's difficult to use any personalization mechanics; we have to increase the size, which eats up screen space

Variability - currently a single banner type is used with a focus on discounts, differing by offer type (PRO+ and EDU). Variability of options is minimal (for example, promoting specific features or tour updates outside of regular tour update scenarios)

Idea

For all users on the Explore screen, replace the banner with a dynamic promo block that we can use for the same sales functions, but not only through discount mechanics, plus it will include free content. For example:

Instead of just a seasonal discount, create a banner with the last visited song and a call to play it as close to the original as possible (with or without a discount)

Show a practice mode "widget" for this song if you're LT and upsell an upgrade to pro+ (access to practice)

Suggest checking how well you sing this song with sing

Recommend a specific course there

Or show a tour update for those who didn't see it on the update

Add a video about official tabs and how to use them

Potentially this will give us more opportunities for personalization and implementation of CRM integration in the app as well, plus it can synergize with personalization of all content on the Explore screen"

### Questions & Answers

Q: What content should be placed in the blocks?

A: Currently, our first viewport displays both a sales promo banner and a Spotify feature promo. We will combine these into a single promo block and place it in the top two positions. We will also add a couple more options to track carousel throughput and conversion.

Q: What will be in the block besides the Sale and Spotify?

A: For the first iteration, we will use a "card" displaying course recommendations and another "card" for the tuner.

Q: Should we create content for the new blocks in the style of Instagram Stories?

A: This will be one of the potential options for scenarios in this block. On the first launch, all cards will function as buttons to open a single screen

# Results

## Decision

Do not roll out either Variant 2 or Variant 3, as the expected uplift in monetization metrics was not observed. Moreover, on Android, a statistically significant decline in CR to Access was identified for Variant 3 .

Android:

Control: 3.48%

Variant 2: 3.21% (-7.9%, p-value = 0.17)

Variant 3: 3.03% (-13%, p-value = 0.023)

IOS:

Control: 6.87%

Variant 2: 6.71% (-2.23%, p-value = 0.57)

Variant 3: 6.69% (-2.55%, p-value = 0.51)

Promo Block CTR vs Monetization:

4–6% of users click on the promo block after exposure.

Overall CTR increases in Variant 3 (but not for Sale block):

iOS: +35% (5.93% vs 4.38%)

Android: +47% (5.54% vs 3.77%)

No corresponding uplift in access or purchases => the CTR uplift represents attention inflation rather than intent uplift. Increased motion drives more clicks, but these interactions are lower quality and do not convert into additional accesses.

Sale & Spotify blocks performance

Spotify purchases decline across both platforms and variants:

iOS: 40 vs 26 vs 32

Android: 18 vs 6 vs 8

Sale purchases drop on Android in all variants; on iOS only Variant 2 shows a small uplift:

iOS: 64 vs 71 vs 59

Android: 34 vs 26 vs 15

Courses & Other purchase source sources

Courses access declines despite an additional entry point in the variant groups

Other sources: stable in Variant 2, decline in Variant 3 (Android)
