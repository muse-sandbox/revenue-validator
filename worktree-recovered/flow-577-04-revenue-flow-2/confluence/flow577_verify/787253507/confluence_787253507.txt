# Pitch

## Initial Context & Idea

Goal: Improve the checkout design, improve the checkout process → access 

Foundation : Create a compact checkout design so that all payment buttons are always visible, especially on small screens

## Final Context & Idea

We are encountering a situation where the payment buttons on the checkout page do not fit within the viewport. Presumably, this could affect the number of people who use these buttons and, ultimately, the number of transactions a user could potentially make using these payment methods:

## Reach & Impact

AVG Daily Metrics A B diff

## Hypothesis

If we make the checkout page more compact and ensure that all payment buttons are clearly visible, we will increase conversion rates by 3% percent, because the people who use these buttons will be able to use them

## Decision 
[macro]

The compact checkout with alternative payment methods placed above the credit card form delivered the strongest monetization results across the experiment

Winning variation showed the highest uplift in revenue metrics: access conversion, charge conversion, Trial → Charge conversion and ARPU, ARPU increased by +15.3% in variation 3

The main driver of revenue growth was a +28.4% uplift in Trial → Charge conversion, resulting in an estimated +$2.2K revenue uplift per day versus control

Moving alternative payment methods higher significantly changed payment behavior: credit cards share dropped from 68.3% to 55.7%, while PayPal usage increased from 12% to 18.7%

At the same time, there has been an increase in charge, which is particularly noticeable in the case of credit cards

here's an idea: encourage even more users to switch to payment buttons by hiding the credit card form behind the button

Rollout group 3 but monitor Charge → Cancel conversion

## Significance analysis

Both test groups show an uplift in monetization metrics: access conversion, charge conversion, Trial → Charge and ARPU. For variation 2, none of the differences are statistically significant; for variation 3, the signal is stronger.

ARPU in variation 3 increased by +15.3%, and statistically signifficant, p = 0.024.

The main contributor to the Revenue lift is a +28.4% increase in Trial → Charge (p = 0.008).

However, significant negative effects in variation 3 are observed downstream of the charge:

The 14-day post-charge cancellation rate increased from 10% to 16.8% (+31.7%, p = 0.043);

7-day retention dropped by −7.2% (p = 0.021), and 14-day retention by −6.5% (p = 0.023).

This indicates that the new checkout converts paid subscriptions more effectively, but the resulting subscribers are more likely to cancel after the first charge and show worse retention on the platform.
