# Research and Context

Goal: We want to monetize our userbase more efficiently by adding extra ad load on users, who spent more than 60 days in our iOS app without purchasing a subscription

Foundation :  We plan to show extra interstitials for 60+ days users (iOS apps) in order to increase revenue without affecting the subscription and product metrics

[macro]
Upside estimations
As for today, we have Interstitials only in iOS apps.

Our current conditions for interstitials are:

maximum impressions - 5 times per calendar day

cooldown between impressions is 180 seconds

Let's estimate revenue upside if we remove all the cooldowns for 60+ days old users.

iOS users older than 60 days generates ~700,000 Tab Views per day.

If we remove all the cooldowns, then each Tab View will generate a potential impression.

Fill rate of interstitials is 0.47 - it means that only in 47 times out of 100 there is a creative for an impression.

That means that we will have 329,000 real impression per day (700,000 * 0.47).

Next our step - is to calclulate potential revenue.

Programmatic CPM of iOS interstitials is 4.83$. 

We will have 329,000 impressions after removing all the cooldowns, so it will bring us +1,589$ (4.83 * 329).

We are ready to calculate the upside now.

Users 60+ days give us ~80% of all the interstitials impressions.

Average daily revenue from iOS Interstitials is about 1,213$.

That means that 60+ days users bring us 971$ daily (1,213 * 0.8).

Thus, our revenue upside will be +618$ (1,589 - 971), or +63% in our 60+days segment.

[/macro]

Estimated revenue upside will be +618$

### Research results

A lot of companies such as Spotify and Duolingo gradually increase user ad load in case users don't buy subscription for a long time. We want to try this approach as well and see whether it'll have effect on the product metrics.

Failed to generate meaningful amount of additional revenue.

During the week of experiment between and we have got +16% in programmatic impressions and -5% in CPM that results in +10% in revenue. We will have +82$ daily upside after rollout, that is dramatically less than the initial expectations.

We have also got some questionable results on product metrics and great drop of NPS metric in this experiment.
