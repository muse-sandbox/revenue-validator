Pitch

Context & Idea

  • Goal: Increase revenue and long-term conversion in Pro+ through systematic optimization of local interstitial paywalls in UG (frequency of impressions, creatives, offers) without compromising retention or cannibalizing existing paying segments.
  • Foundation: Local interstitials are now displayed according to strict rules (once a day instead of the usual ad interstitial and then only as a fallback), sell one standard intro offer Pro+ (19.99 for the first year → 39.99), do not take into account the segment and context, and are practically not tested as a separate monetization channel. As a result, we are not utilizing their potential: we do not know the optimal frequency and timing, we do not check the impact of different creatives/formats and offers on conversion and retention, and we cannot consciously balance subscription vs. advertising.
  • Idea: Understand whether we can significantly improve key metrics for local interstitial paywalls (subscription conversion, revenue, paying traffic quality) by consciously working with display frequency, screen content, and offers, rather than leaving the current static implementation as is.

Questions & Answers

Frequency and logic of displays

  • How often are local interstitials currently displayed for different segments and platforms?

  • Which display frequency converts free and paid segments (purchase, trial, intro) most often?

  • Is it possible to reduce the number of impressions without a significant drop in conversion and revenue (are there any “extra” impressions)?

  • Is there a combination of timing and frequency that generates more revenue with comparable retention and advertising ARPU?

First screen and display format

  • Does gamification (e.g., the “swipe to reveal offer” mechanic) increase conversion and/or the quality of paying traffic compared to the current screen?

  • If showing a video before the main paywall, are there video options that consistently perform better than the current pre-paywall?

  • Can changing the appearance of the interstitial from show to show (variable design, different messages) increase conversion without increasing cancellations and cannibalizing other paid streams?

Offer and pricing

  • Can we increase the price of the current intro offer without significant losses in conversion and without an increase in cancellations after the first year?
  • Can we shorten the length of the intro period without losing conversion and without increasing cancellations?
  • Are there combinations of display time and discount level (e.g., increasing the discount with each subsequent display) that increase conversion and LTV without worsening churn and advertising revenue?

Ex-premium and reactivation

  • Why do ex-premium users convert so well from interstitial ads: how do they differ in terms of behavior and subscription history?

  • What subscriptions did they have before (type of rights: Pro / Unified / Courses / Sing; subscription type: paid / trial / intro)?

  • What percentage of users in this group never paid at all (only took a trial and canceled before billing)?

  • How long ago did their previous subscription end, and how much time usually passes between cancellation and a new purchase through interstitial ads?

  • How is the frequency/context of interstitial display related to the likelihood of reactivation in this group (is there a “sweet spot” in terms of time or number of displays)?

Previous interstitial experiments

  • [2025-04-11] UG App: paywall – offer instead of ad interstitials [2025-07-10]

    • The 2025 project stalled at the design stage: the uplifta model based on ARPU/Revenue was prepared, but the A/B test was not launched and no final results were obtained.

  • [2025-07-07] UG iOS: paywall – paywall after ad interstitial [2025-08-12]

    • After each ad-interstitial was shown, a two-step pre-paywall focused on the ad-free experience was shown, followed by the standard Pro+ paywall, repeating this scenario for all free users (including ex-premium).

    • The test did not yield the expected increase in conversion in access (~+10% according to the model): due to a sharp drop on the first pre-paywall, the final conversion of the source fell to ~0.07%, and the hypothesis was deemed unsuccessful. The team recorded the status as FAIL and decided to focus on projects to replace some of the ad interstitials with their banners and videos.

  • [2025-07-09] UG App: paywall – monetization video instead of ad interstitials [2025-XX-XX]

    • We tested video monetization instead of the usual ad interstitial for users who are not shown ads.

    • Iteration 2 (#6428, iOS+Android) showed significant ARPU growth without a noticeable impact on retention: the uplift was positive and statistically significant, so it was decided to roll out the solution more widely. The learnings showed that the short UG video format with a clear “Try for free” message works better than static images, but requires a strict limit on the frequency of displays and a careful trigger so as not to annoy users.

Description of the Solution & Mockups

Solution

true1 iteration - interstitial with code

  • The experiment will work for iOS and Android users.

  • The experiment will only work for new users who have already completed the tour install during the experiment.

  • The experiment is available only to the free audience (there is no subscription and never has been)

  • We are replacing our standard local interstitial with a new one featuring a promotional code.

    • This new type of interstitial will only be available when the user first enters the tab, and they will only see it once.

    • The interstitial has a 5-second timer, after which it can be skipped

    • The experiment has a version C; the only difference is that this version removes the timer and the option to skip the interstitial.

  • On the new interstitial screen, the user will see an interactive element - a promo code.

    • The promo code is initially hidden, but the user can rub it off to reveal the discount (like a lottery ticket).

    • When the promo code appears, we will launch a small animation (confetti or something else that emphasizes the luckiness of this offer).

    • After that, the user sees a paywall with the discount received.

    • No additional actions (like entering a code) are required—the discount is automatically applied for eligible users.

    • The user's device vibrates during the “erasing” of the promo code.

  • The products on the new screen are similar to our usual products in interstitials. The only difference here is the scratching mechanic instead of a static screen.
