# Research and Context

Goal: Use ads in the app as a way to attract people to subscribe and increase the conversion to access

Foundation: Right now, we don't specifically advertise our subscription as being ad-free. All the entry points are about features or premium content. However, we reach around 100к uniq interstitial viewers a day on iOS, which makes it a potentially good spot for selling subscriptions. Interstitial ads are currently only displayed in the iOS app.

# Hypothesis

If we show a paywall after the ads, focusing on an ad-free experience, then the conversion to access will increase by 10%. This is because we'll make the paywall more noticeable and communicate with the audience that an ad-free experience is available to them

# Design

[macro]
Red Fail
[/macro]

We're not rolling out, as we were unable to achieve the expected increase in access

The main problem is the drop-off from the first pre-paywall in the new scenario, resulting in a very low conversion rate of the source (only 0.07%)

We could try to make the entire funnel leak-proof and lead all users to the paywall, but most likely, we won't get any additional access there, as the source cannibalizes other entry points on the tab.

We won't continue with this hypothesis. We'll focus on the project that involves replacing interstitial ads with our banners and videos.

## Decision

Results

We expected to achieve 10% growth in subscriptions through the new source, but ultimately the difference between variations was around 0.

In the new scenario, conversion to subscription was only 0.07% of those who saw the new splash

As a result, the share of subscriptions from the new source accounted for 3% of all subscriptions, but at the same time it cannibalized other sources from the tab page

In the new scenario, 96% of users leave at the very first pre-paywall, indicating there are problems with paywall reach

Conclusion

Longer funnel with pre paywall screen works worse than just banner

As a next step, we can make all pre-paywalls in the new scenario unskippable. This will solve the problem of high churn and low paywall visibility
