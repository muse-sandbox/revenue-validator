# Research and Context

We want to sell rich ad experience to our advertisers in the MU App. In order to do this, we want to show interstitials from our direct advertisers (Advertisers from the music industry). We're planning to implement them into the MU App

The number of expected impressions for nonpaying users in apps from US, CA, AU and Europe is about ~20,000 per day.

https://redash.ultimate-guitar.com/queries/21605/source#43287

Showing interstitials for this users on each pageview will bring us 110,000 impressions daily
