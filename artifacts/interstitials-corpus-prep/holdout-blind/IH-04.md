# IH-04 — Instant discounted purchase replacing trial/intro offers on both interstitial surfaces

## Product context
The Ultimate Guitar app runs two high-traffic local interstitial surfaces: a free-user interstitial (which today sells a discounted intro plan, ~$19.99 for the first period) and a winback interstitial for former subscribers (which today offers a fresh 14-day trial rolling into ~$39.99). Both fire in the once-per-day interstitial slot and in the fallback shown when an ad fails to load. This experiment swaps the offer on both surfaces — triggers and creative structure untouched — for a single instant discounted premium plan at $29.99: no trial, no intro period, the purchase happens immediately. The bet is on subscriber quality: asking for money up front should filter for buyers with genuine intent.

## Pre-launch research & rationale
The idea is an explicit follow-up to two earlier live tests on these exact surfaces, both of which grew revenue via trials/discounts but degraded quality (early cancels +69–118%, AOV/ARPPU −9–14%), and whose own next-steps sections called for dropping the trial and promoting direct purchase:

1. **Free-user interstitial offer test (earlier).** A 14-day-trial version failed on quality (trial→charge conversion −36% to −54%); switching to an intro plan worked (ARPU +11.5% iOS / +25% Android). Key learning: trials pull in low-intent users.
2. **Winback interstitial test (earlier).** Fresh 14-day trial → ~$39.99 produced strong ARPU (+42.8%) but early cancellations spiked +69–118% — the trial attracted re-trialists who churn fast.
3. **Most relevant precedent — instant cheap plan for a narrow young-user segment (earlier).** A cheap instant plan with no trial for under-25 users lifted offer-funnel conversion 2.5–3x, source revenue +49–105%, source ARPU +50%, while cancel/refund rates did not rise and often fell (charge→14d refund −41%). Direct evidence that a cheaper instant price can raise conversion without degrading quality. This project goes one step further: an even cheaper instant offer on the two high-traffic interstitial surfaces for all free users plus ex-subscribers.

**Reach (restricted to users who completed the install tour during the run and then hit an interstitial):** ~12,950 eligible new users/day — iOS free-surface ~6,143, iOS winback ~504, Android free-surface ~5,983, Android winback ~323. This is ~4–12x smaller than the full interstitial pool, which is the binding constraint on power. Baselines were recomputed on a mature net-revenue cohort (2026-06-01..06-23), attributed by funnel source; new-install users convert ~2–4x better than the full pool, so an earlier full-pool baseline had understated conversion and ARPU.

**Modeled A/B funnel (avg/day at 100% rollout, instant $29.99 model).** Free surface: iOS ~6,294 members/day, members→buyer 0.67% held flat, ARPPU modeled $12.99→$20.99, ARPU $0.087→$0.14 (+61.6%), revenue $551→$890/day; Android ~6,094 members/day, 0.33% flat, ARPPU $15.31→$25.49, ARPU $0.051→$0.085 (+66.5%), revenue $312→$520. Winback surface: iOS ~500 members/day, members→buyer 1.41%→2.65% (+88.3% modeled from removing the trial step), ARPPU $25.06→$20.99 (down by design), ARPU $0.35→$0.56 (+57.7%), revenue $176→$278; Android ~300 members/day, 1.68%→3.17% (+88.3%), ARPPU $28.54→$25.49, ARPU $0.48→$0.81 (+68.2%), revenue $144→$242. Total: ARPU +60.7% iOS / +67.0% Android, revenue $727→$1,168 (iOS) and $456→$762 (Android) per day. Control winback funnel decomposition motivating the trial removal: members→trial start 7.2% iOS / 9.5% Android, then only 19.5% / 17.7% of trials convert to a charge — the instant offer cuts out that leaky trial stage entirely.

**Forecast framing:** steady-state surface revenue on the restricted audience ≈ $463/day; a +10% ARPU floor would add ~+$46/day, while the full modeled effect is ≈ +$747/day (+63%) at 100% rollout, driven by the free surface. The absolute value is small because the eligible audience is tiny; the stated value of the experiment is validating the offer for a later full rollout. Explicitly flagged two-way price risk: winback price DOWN $39.99→$29.99 (−25%) plus trial removal means winback conversion must rise >33% to break even on ARPPU; free-surface first payment UP $19.99→$29.99 (+50%) means conversion/volume may fall.

## Hypothesis
As stated pre-launch: replacing the current trial / intro-discount offers with one instant discounted premium plan for all free users (free-surface interstitial) and former subscribers (winback interstitial), in both the daily slot and the ad-failure fallback, is expected to raise ARPU by at least XX% (numeric floor left unfilled in the hypothesis sentence; the modeled expectation was ≈ +60–67% total ARPU) without hurting subscriber quality (charge→14d cancel/refund and 7-day retention) — the reasoning being that a purchase with no trial safety net and no discount ladder attracts buyers with real intent, as the earlier instant-discount precedent showed (conversion 2.5–3x up with cancel/refund flat or lower).

## Mechanics
- **Placement:** both existing local interstitial surfaces — the free-user interstitial and the winback interstitial (interstitial, tab, song, and search placements in the funnel naming).
- **Trigger / Timing:** unchanged from current logic — the once-daily interstitial slot and the ad-failure fallback; activation fires on the first tab entry of the day once the user is eligible.
- **Frequency:** unchanged from current interstitial frequency rules.
- **Audience segment:** only users who completed the install tour during the experiment; within that, free users (free surface) and formerly subscribed, currently inactive users (winback surface).
- **Platforms:** iOS and Android.
- **Offer-SKU structure:** a single instant discounted premium plan at $29.99 replaces both the ~$19.99 intro plan (free users) and the 14-day trial → ~$39.99 (winback). No trial, no intro ramp; subscribe = immediate charge.

## Experiment design
- **Arms:** 3 (A control / B / C), with Bonferroni-adjusted α/2 = 0.025 built into the power estimates.
- **Primary (goal) metric:** Total ARPU on the whole eligible surface, analyzed with CUPED. Modeled lift +60.7% (iOS) / +67.0% (Android), powering in roughly 4–7 days.
- **Proxy metrics:** free-surface ARPU (baselines $0.087 iOS / $0.051 Android; modeled lifts +61.6% / +66.5%; MDE 0.048 / 0.036; sample per variation 8,131 / 14,644; ~4 / ~8 days) and winback members→buyer conversion (baselines 1.41% iOS / 1.68% Android; modeled lift +88.3% both; MDE 0.089 / 0.098; sample per variation 2,387 / 1,988; ~15 / ~20 days). Total-ARPU goal cells: baselines $0.11 iOS / $0.071 Android, MDE 0.047 / 0.038, samples 8,511 / 13,234, ~4 / ~7 days. Power 0.8, alpha 0.025 throughout.
- **Guardrails:** charge→14d cancel and refund rates, 7-day retention, ARPPU/AOV (expected down by design on the winback surface — judge winback on ARPU/quality, not ARPPU), trial share, and free-surface members→buyer conversion (expected flat; watch it does not drop under the +50% price).
- **Planned duration:** binding duration ≈ 20 days, set by the winback conversion cell (the "long pole"); the Total-ARPU goal and the free surface read out in about a week. A pre-launch design review concluded the experiment is powerable in ~3 weeks — an earlier "unpowerable / ~100 days" verdict was an artifact of a mismeasured (full-pool) baseline — and that the new-install eligibility restriction raises intent/quality without gutting power. Pooling iOS + Android was reserved as a fallback if a winback cell lagged.
