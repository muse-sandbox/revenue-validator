# IH-03 — Song-contextual message tailoring on the interstitial surface

## Product context
Most free and ex-paid users of the Ultimate Guitar app see one generic full-screen local interstitial paywall, weakly tied to what they are actually doing in the app. This experiment is the first iteration of a planned system of behavior-tailored interstitial messaging, where the message and framing are chosen from user context (e.g. the song being opened, repeated behavior patterns, monetization eligibility) instead of a single one-size-fits-all upsell. The iteration tested here personalizes only the interstitial's message/creative — anchored on the song the user just opened — while keeping the commercial offers, eligibility rules, and downstream paywall exactly as they are today.

## Pre-launch research & rationale
Confidence case assembled by the team:

1. **Internal — segmented/behavioral interstitials outperform generic ones.** An earlier winback interstitial program for former subscribers delivered roughly +42–43% ARPU versus control and up to 10–20x higher conversion than the generic free-user interstitial, suggesting that segment- and history-aware targeting converts far better than broad, uniform upsells.
2. **Internal — repetition of the same generic screen decays fast.** Analysis of local interstitials showed 60–90% of conversions occur within the first 2–6 impressions, after which effectiveness drops sharply — an argument for varying message/offer by behavior rather than repeating the same screen.
3. **Internal documentation** of the local-interstitial monetization layer notes performance depends heavily on matching placement, frequency, and audience definition to user context.
4. **Industry.** A subscription-infrastructure vendor's analysis of contextual paywalls finds that paywalls launched off a concrete in-app action reliably beat static generic screens, because they meet the user at the moment of highest motivation; a paywall-personalization vendor reports behavior-driven personalized paywalls as a standard lever for subscription conversion and LTV; an experimentation vendor's writing on app personalization likewise holds that generic, unsegmented experiences lose to behavior-based personalization.

**Reach baselines (current state; users without an active subscription who repeatedly opened the tab of the same song within a 2-week window):**
- 3+ opens of the same song: iOS — free 33,378 users (interstitial-source subscriptions 28; member→access 0.084%; ~22 charges; ~$554; ARPPU ~$25; ARPU ~$0.017), ex-subscribers 6,232 (46 subs; 0.74%; ~13 charges; ~$345; ARPU ~$0.055), ex-long-trial 279 (4 subs; 1.43%; ARPU ~$0.10). Android — free 21,216 (17 subs; 0.080%; ~14 charges; ~$440; ARPU ~$0.021), ex-subscribers 2,303 (33 subs; 1.43%; ~$232; ARPU ~$0.10), ex-long-trial 167 (4 subs; 2.40%; ARPU ~$0.16).
- 2+ opens: iOS — free 48,260 (42 subs; 0.087%; ~$839; ARPU ~$0.017), ex-subscribers 9,748 (69 subs; 0.71%; ~$574; ARPU ~$0.059), ex-long-trial 404 (8 subs; 1.98%); Android — free 29,923 (24 subs; 0.080%; ~$580; ARPU ~$0.019), ex-subscribers 3,442 (43 subs; 1.25%; ~$307; ARPU ~$0.089), ex-long-trial 228 (5 subs; 2.19%).

The pitch judged reach as signal-dependent (different personalization signals cover different audience shares), impact as flowing to revenue/ARPU rather than traffic, and effort as moderate since existing interstitial and offer infrastructure is reused with a new personalization layer on top. An open pre-launch question was the ratio of the three audience segments (free users; payers who already used the 2-week trial; payers who had not).

## Hypothesis
As stated pre-launch: swapping the current generic interstitial for a personalized one whose message and framing are tailored to the user's context — with commercial offers and eligibility rules kept exactly as today — is expected to make the interstitial layer more efficient (higher 14-day ARPU and higher conversion to the premium tier) versus control, on the reasoning that a tailored message at a high-intent moment should beat a uniform screen. No numeric expected lift was quantified in the document.

## Mechanics
- **Placement:** local interstitial slot on the song/tab surface; the new context-tailored screen takes precedence over other interstitials when the user is eligible.
- **Trigger:** fires when the user opens a song and becomes eligible for an interstitial impression in that placement; the opened song is the contextual anchor of the message.
- **Timing / Frequency:** the context-tailored screen appears only once per user; afterwards the user reverts to the standard interstitial logic.
- **Audience segment:** both free users and ex-paid users; each segment receives the product and copy matching its existing monetization eligibility (free users get the standard free-flow product configuration; former subscribers eligible for the extra 2-week trial get the winback version with that trial).
- **Platforms:** iOS and Android (with a prerequisite tech change to the Android interstitial source naming before launch).
- **Offer-SKU structure:** unchanged commercial logic — this tests message personalization, not a new offer model. Creative: a headline urging the user to play the just-opened song like its original artist, a supporting line about learning it closer to the original with official tabs and tools, plus a segment-specific badge (a steep percentage discount on the premium tier for free users; a welcome-back extra 2-week premium trial for ex-paid users).

## Experiment design
- **Arms (2):** A — control, current generic interstitial (per-segment as today); B — the song-contextual tailored screen, with per-segment creatives (free vs ex-paid).
- **Primary metrics:** interstitial-attributed conversion to the premium tier and 14-day ARPU vs control.
- **Analysis segments:** all users; users whose subscription came from an interstitial source; free users.
- **Power / MDE / planned duration:** no formal power calculation was documented pre-launch (the design table remained an unfilled template).
- **Instrumentation plan:** activation event in all variations at eligibility; existing splash and paywall/purchase funnel events sent with new source values distinguishing the free-flow and winback-flow variants of the tailored screen; product parameters to be verified.
